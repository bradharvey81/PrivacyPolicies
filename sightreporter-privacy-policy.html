Privacy Policy

This policy explains what SightReporter does — and, just as importantly, does not do — with your information. The app is built so your site photos, locations, and voice notes stay on your device unless you deliberately turn on a cloud feature.

Effective 25 July 2026 Applies to the SightReporter Android app

At a glance

  • Your data stays on your device by default. Photos, GPS tags, notes and ratings are stored in the app's private storage on your phone.
  • Voice notes never leave your phone. Dictation uses your device's built-in offline speech recognition; audio is turned into text on the device and is not recorded or uploaded.
  • Cloud backup is optional and opt-in. Nothing is uploaded unless you turn on Google Drive backup, the gallery copy, or a local backup folder yourself.
  • The free version shows ads; there are no accounts. Ads come from Google AdMob. Upgrading to Pro removes them. We never sell your data.

1. Who we are

SightReporter (“the app”, “we”, “us”) is an Android app for creating site-visit photo reports. The app is published by Brad Harvey. If you have any questions about this policy or your data, contact us at brad81@gmail.com.

We do not operate our own servers or user accounts. The app runs on your device, and any data that leaves your device goes only to the Google services you choose to enable, described below.

3. Permissions & why

The app asks only for the permissions it needs to do its job. You can grant or revoke these in your device settings at any time.

  • Camera — to take the photos in your reports.
  • Location (precise) — to tag photos with their GPS position and show it on the report. Decline it and the app still works; photos simply won't be geo-tagged.
  • Microphone — for on-device voice-to-text notes. Audio is not recorded or uploaded.
  • Internet & network state — used only to upload reports to your own Google Drive (if you enable it) and to show ads in the free version.
  • Storage (on older Android versions) — to save an optional copy of your photos to your device gallery when you turn that on.

4. Optional cloud & backup features

These are all off until you switch them on. Each sends data only to a destination you control.

Google Drive backup

If you enable Drive backup, the app uploads your generated reports (PDF and Word documents) and their photos to a folder named “SightReporter” in your own Google Drive. The app uses Google's restricted drive.file access, which means it can only see and manage the files it creates — it cannot read the rest of your Drive. This data is held in your Google account and governed by Google's Privacy Policy.

Save a copy to your gallery

You can choose to copy captured photos into a public “SightReporter” album on your device. If you have Google Photos (or another gallery-backup app) set to back up that folder, those apps will then handle the photos under their own privacy policies and your account settings.

Local backup folder

You can back up your reports to a folder you choose on the device or removable storage. That copy stays wherever you put it; it is not sent to us or anyone else.

5. Advertising (free tier)

The free version of the app displays ads supplied by Google AdMob. To serve ads, Google's advertising technology may collect and process information such as your device's advertising identifier, general device information, and app interactions. This processing is carried out by Google, not by us, and is described in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

You can limit ad personalisation in your device's settings (Google → Ads), or remove ads entirely by upgrading to Pro.

6. In-app purchases

SightReporter Pro is a one-time in-app purchase that unlocks unlimited photos per report, removes ads, removes the trial watermark on PDFs, and adds an editable Word copy of each report. Purchases are processed by Google Play Billing. We do not receive or store your payment card details — Google handles the transaction under its own terms and privacy policy.

7. Who your data is shared with

We do not sell your data, and we do not share it with advertisers or data brokers. The only third parties involved are Google services that act on your instruction or your device:

  • Google Drive — only if you enable backup, and only into your own account.
  • Google Play Billing — to process a Pro purchase.
  • Google AdMob — to display ads in the free version.
  • Your device's speech service — to convert voice to text on-device.

We may disclose information if required to by law, but since we hold no personal data on any server of ours, there is generally nothing for us to disclose.

8. Keeping & deleting your data

Your reports and photos stay on your device until you remove them. You are in control:

  • Delete individual items in the app; deleted photos go to a Trash you can empty.
  • Uninstall the app to remove all of its on-device data.
  • Cloud copies — anything you uploaded to Google Drive, or that Google Photos backed up, must be removed from those services separately, as they are in your account, not ours.

9. Security

Your reports are stored in the app's private storage, which other apps cannot read. Uploads to Google Drive use encrypted (HTTPS) connections. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure, but because the app keeps your data on your device by default, the surface for exposure is deliberately small.

10. Children

SightReporter is a professional tool intended for site inspections and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

11. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR in the EU/UK, or the CCPA in California), you may have rights to access, correct, or delete your personal data, and to object to certain processing. Because your report data lives on your device, you can exercise most of these rights directly in the app or through your device and Google account settings. For anything else, contact us at brad81@gmail.com and we will help.
